Post by leovii » Fri Apr 30, 2021 3:51 pm

Hi if new playing wib wob or using good wib wob tactics is a good way to start the game and learn about tactics and player positioning Etc

The wib wob the ai uses is generally not as strong as what an experienced human player can create and is very beatable with solid non wib wob tactics

Others may disagree but I see wib wob and non wib wob as completely different ways of playing the game.

Wib wob will help you understand player positioning more as a beginner and non wib wob will force you to understand how to maximise player and team Instructions more

It entirely depends how you want to play the game as a good wib wob tactic can become very plug n play and non wib wob is more adaptable in comparison by easier tweaks in game and adapting more structured tactics for both defence and attack but is more challenging

Post by samsami » Mon Sep 13, 2021 12:09 pm

On the old website there was a post by Patinoz or churky which explained WibWob in relatively simple terms.

Here's an edited and simplified version of the first post:


I'll present a quick and simple way to play this game in order to win anything you want without using a super tactic.

This is not the only way you can be successful, far from it. It's just one of the simplest ways to achieve great things.

Tactics
In order to be successful in this game, all you need is take into consideration a few things about the match engine in general and apply a few simple things to the WibWob screens.

1. Use a narrow formation, preferably without wingers. When using a narrow formation you're already one step ahead.
2. Don't play with 3 at the back, use 4. If you don't mind unrealistic tactics, use 2 defenders as the game doesn't cope with that too well.
3. Employ a DMC to your formation because it's the most overpowered position in CM 01/02.
4. Use either 1 or 3 strikers, simply because it works better in this game.

Team instructions
Use short passing, pressing ON, offside trap ON, attacking mentality, tackling hard. You cannot go wrong with those settings.

Without the ball screen
The lowest middle WOB box is the key, you need to fill it with bodies.

On upper WOB screens, you need to push your defensive line much higher than the default position. It will make you both defensively (offside trap) and offensively (pressing) potent.

With the ball screen
Put your fullbacks (or other wide players if you decided to use them) as narrow as possible while maintaining their wide positions on the overview screen.
Crowd the highest middle WIB box with your forwards and (attacking) midfielders.
Place your players on the edges of each box, avoid having more than one player in the middle.

That's it. Just by following those simple instructions you can improve any non-WibWob tactic by a considerable margin.

Post by LeeYoungPyo777 » Sat Nov 29, 2025 5:04 pm

here are my thoughts on wibwob:
it's not "cheating", unless you just implement an already-made tactic. but if you learn the fundamentals of wibwob, it's like going on a tactics course - it actually makes sense if you think about it, box the midfield, ask your players to be in particular positions on the field when you have the ball, be more aggressive with the ball and more pragmatic without - why would a default tactic do this already for you? it won't. so tweak it - top managers in football do it.

Now, wibwob or no wibwob, you still need the fundamentals of management: a good eye for players, playing players in the right positions, in-game management (responding to what the opposition is doing, do you mark a key player for e.g? or do you go normal mentality or attacking, etc); keeping an eye on the weather, is it wet is it cold, in-game fitness; keeping team morale high, selling the right players, buying the right players, keeping your team consistent, implementing the correct training, sending players to physio if necessary and managing that, building a squad so you have sufficient cover, signing the right backroom staff. so you still need the basis of a good team with the right attributes, wibwob or no wibwob, but the overall thinking is that you have to evolve from the default tactics in some way if you really want to master the game.
