Title
[DOCUMENTATION] Ultimate League Guide.

Category
Documentation.

Created By
Leclercq.

Description
A guide to the original version of Championship Manager 2001/2002 developed by two members of the champman0102 community. This is truly an ultimate guide as it covers every playable nation in the game, the finances at each club, their attendance levels, the training, reputation and board levels and also covers the structure of each league and competition. For good measure, the duo have also provided a list of the best 11 players in each country!
