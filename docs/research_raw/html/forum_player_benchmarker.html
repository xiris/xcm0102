PlayerBenchmarker

  • Quote

Post by agevak » Sat Oct 12, 2024 6:23 pm

I play CM series since 90s and always ask myself a question: which attributes are really important and at which degree.
This forum is a great resource, but even here I've failed to find enough information about this article. Many people discuss this important aspect, but usually don't provide any reasoning.
However, here are awesome tools for patching, editing and benchmarking the game. So I've decided to finally get real proven knowledge of what the attributes are about right from the game itself...
So, now on we have a tool for benchmarking player attributes. It's based on CM0102 Tactics Benchmarker, it allows editing attributes for a bunch of players (grouped by positions) and run them to play several seasons in Greek league.
Then it aggregates the results and appends average values to a file. Thus, we can analyze the outcome to see which attributes are more important and which are useless.
The tool is raw (say alpha version) but since I don't have time to polish it and make it more user-friendly, decided to release it as is. Here is a link to GitHub: https://github.com/agevak/CM0102PlayerBenchmarker
There is a readme file describing how to use it. Source is available, so anybody can modify it as he likes.
Here I publish the results of my testing. Reader is encouraged to perform his own tests.

Testing base.
1) Used CM version is the one from CM0102 Tactics Benchmarker v1.1 with most critical patches applied via Nick's patcher (fix of creativity, positioning and marking bugs).
2) Took the PAS Giannina club as it was in the CM0102 Tactics Benchmarker v1.1.
3) Split all players into the categories explained below. The program edited position values for each player accordingly (e.g. for "attacking midfielder" set Midfield, Attacking Midfielder and Center values to 20) in order to avoid positional penalties (which made Versatility attribute useless BTW).
4) Set for all players Natural Fitness to 20 and Injury Proneness to 1 (though CM0102 Tactics Benchmarker v1.1 says that injuries are disabled, but just in case).
5) Used mostly my own tactics (https://github.com/agevak/CM0102Tactics ... usters.tct), but sometimes (e.g. for GK testing) used weak default 4-4-2 tactic.
6) Run 40–100 seasons per test. The program captures only these stats (IMO, everything else is not important in the end of the day):
- Average Goals Scored per season,
- Average Goals Conceded per season,
- Average Points earned per season.
7) I've concentrated on the team's Goals Scored and Goals Conceded only, didn't play any attention on players' stats. Points earned always correlated with scored and conceded goals very tightly.

Captain
Spoiler
The only thing useful for captain is Influence. Good captain helps a lot, and it's not difficult to find 1 player with 18-20 influence, so just have such one in your squad and select him to be a captain.
Simple attributes
Spoiler
"Simple" attributes are those which are not affected by Current Ability. The effect of such an attribute falls in one of these categories:
1) Linear. Say Pace of 5 vs 1 leads to +1 more goal scored, 10 vs 1 - to +2 goals, 15 vs 1 - 10 +3, 20 vs 1 - to +4. Not necessary a perfectly linear effect, but somewhat close to it.
Bright example: Pace.
2) Penalty. Low values lead to a big penalty, while very high values provide no to little pros over average values.
Bright example: technique and work rate for attacking midfielders.
3) Bonus. Average values don't give advantage over low ones, but only high values do.
Bright example: Jumping for forwards.
I've tested simple attributes mostly in 20 vs 1 scenario, didn't play much with values in the middle. Only noticed the abovementioned examples.
Natural fitness and Injury Proneness
These 2 attributes work exactly as regular player can expect. So I will not mention them further, please just keep them in mind. Their effect is very high for all positions. Natural Fitness of 1 is deadly even for GK.

Creativity and Passing
Spoiler
Creativity gives no effect without great passing. Don't know the exact formula, but as a rule of thumb: assume Creativity = Min(Creativity, Passing).
If Creativity is not in the list of important attributes for some position, then it doesn't give any improve, even if Passing does.
There is an in match effect of high Creativity (even if not supported by high Passing) - huge amount of passes. However, this effect doesn't really help to score more goals by team neither to concede less.
Super tacklers
Spoiler
These things help to make huge amount of tackle attempts in matches:
1) Aggression and Bravery (only work together, not separately)
2) Tackling (also improves tackle success rate)
3) Positioning
However, among these things, the only useful one for the team (to concede fewer goals and often to score more as well) is the Positioning.
GK
Spoiler
In decreasing order of importance (order inside a group is random):
1) Handling.
2) Aggression.
Agility (seems to have "penalty" effect with a milestone around 12).
Jumping.
Positioning.
3) Work rate.
Reflexes (very specific, see below).
Negative effect attributes (i.e. the lower, the better):
1) Anticipation (slight effect).
Handling and Reflexes intrinsic must sum up to 0-20 (usually very close to 0, but some very lucky GKs has up to 20), otherwise (if modified via save editor) game will correct them to be so. Handling is much preferred over Reflexes.
Best GK in ODB is David Dei with his Handling intrinsic around 45 and Reflexes around -25.
DC
Spoiler
DC without arrow.
Most important:
1) Positioning
About 2 times less important:
2) Marking
Less important:
3) Jumping (effect is not "bonus")
4) Stamina
Strength
5) Dirtiness (the lower, the better of course)
Other simple attributes also affect, but each of them separately affects very slightly. Other ability-dependent attributes are completely useless.
Marking makes sense only for players who having Man marking instruction. If marking is Zonal or None, Marking is completely useless.
In my experience of tactics benchmarking, Man marking instruction is the best for DCs, but not for others. Thus, put Marking attribute on 2nd place for DC.
Winger
Spoiler
DL or DR without arrow.
With arrow, the situation is mostly similar, though attacking attributes become more important.
In decreasing order of importance (order inside a group is random):
1) Dribbling (to score more)
Positioning (to concede less)
Technique (to score more, effect is "penalty", with value >= 10 penalty becomes close to zero)
Teamwork (mostly for scoring)
2) Stamina
Passing (Creativity doesn't help, even for ML/MR -> AML/AMR)
3) Determination
Pace
Off The Ball (to score more)
4) Jumping
Workrate (to concede less)
No other attribute makes significant effect alone.
For more attacking winger positions (like ML/MR with arrow to AML/AMR) I didn't make much testing, but there is definitely a difference, at least noticed that Positioning becomes useless.
DMC
Spoiler
DMC without arrow.
In decreasing order of importance (order inside a group is random):
1) Positioning (to both score more and concede less)
2) Agility (mostly to concede less)
Determination (to score more)
Off The Ball (to score more)
Dribbling (to score more)
Passing (to score more)
3) Professionalism
Aggression
Creativity
Slight effect:
4) Sportsmanship (to conceded less)
Technique (to score more)
No other attribute makes significant effect alone.
AMC
Spoiler
MC with arrow to AMC.
In decreasing order of importance (order inside a group is random):
1) Technique (to score more, effect is "penalty", with value >= 15 penalty becomes close to zero)
Workrate (to concede less, effect is "penalty", with value >= 10 penalty becomes close to zero)
Positioning (mostly to concede less, but to score more also)
2) Dribbling (to score more)
3) Passing (to score more)
Off the ball (to score more)
Creativity (to score more)
3) Pace
Jumping
Determination
Flair (to score more)
4) Consistency
Important matches
Professionalism
Strength
No other attribute makes significant effect alone.
Effect of Off The Ball becomes smaller when using great FCs.
AM->FC
Spoiler
AMC with arrow to FC.
In decreasing order of importance (order inside a group is random):
1) Off the ball
2) Technique (to score more, effect is "penalty", with value >= 15 penalty becomes close to zero)
Jumping ("bonus" effect, value <= 10 provides no bonus)
Dribbling (to score more)
Positioning (to both score more and concede less)
Passing (Creativity doesn't help)
3) Pace (to both score more and concede less, "linear" effect)
Workrate (to both score more and concede less)
Stamina
4) Flair
Professionalism (mostly to concede less)
Heading (to score more)
Teamwork
Very slight effect:
5) Acceleration
Agility
Aggression and Bravery
Balance
Determination
Sportsmanship
Strength
No other attribute makes significant effect alone. All simply attributes together 20 vs 1 make effect comparable to Technique 15 vs 1.
FC
Spoiler
FC without arrow.
In decreasing order of importance (order inside a group is random):
1) Jumping ("bonus" effect)
Off the ball
2) Determination
Heading
Passing
Technique
Work rate
3) Consistency
Important matches
Flair
Pace
Professionalism
Sportsmanship
Stamina
Strength
Jumping 20 is much better than 18, while 15 is slightly better than 1.
All (or most) other simple attributes also have progressive effect.
Ability dependent attributes (expect for Off The Ball) require big increase (+60..+80 intrinsic) to make effect similar to 10 -> 20 transition from a simple attribute.
Conclusion
Attributes are quite sophisticated and this is one of the reasons why we love CM so much.
I encourage you to perform tests on your own, because I believe there are many aspects which I've missed.
Spoiler
Big surprise is that Finishing, Tackling and Crossing have no effect at all. This is probably a bug in the game. Or maybe they are used in some specific relation with other attributes (like Creativity with Passing).

About "supertacklers": only Positioning and Marking (if marking is set to "man") really matter. Tackling, Aggresion, Bravery only increase (drastically) tackles per match and tackles success %. But tackles are just cosmetic numbers, they don't lead to less conceded goals nor to more scored goals.
This conclusion is made according to benchmarking, nothing else. Obviously it contradicts with real life and common sense.

About finishing...
According to benchmarking. Finish is completely useless. It doesn't increase scored goals regardless of Off the ball.
I.e. with low off the ball, raising Finishing changes nothing. With high Off the ball, raising Finish also changes nothing. This is a combination I've tried. Unless there is some other magic combination, there is a bug in the game.

Last year I've digged into disassembly and found 2 functions, located nearby each other, very similar functions. One of them uses Heading and another one uses Finishing.
Patching conditional jumps in the Heading one lead to many scored goals with records like "goal!!! wonderful via header by Crespo..." comments (wording is different, I just state the idea).
However, similar patch of the Finishing function changed nothing.
I've failed to find the bug, but I think it exists.
