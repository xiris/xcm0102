CM 01/02 Training Guide: The Ultimate Routine for Your Squad – CM0102 – Tips Skip to content 

Setting up training in Championship Manager 01/02 might feel time-consuming, but it is a game-changer. 

A structured routine ensures your wonderkids reach their full potential and your veterans maintain their physical stats longer. 

Credits to Kingsley (Champman) and Joe the Greebo for the technical insights! 

Understanding Attribute Impact 

Fitness: Acceleration, agility, jumping, endurance, strength, and balance. 

Tactics: Decisions, marking, off the ball, positioning, and teamwork. 

Shooting: Finishing and long shots. 

Skill: Anticipation, heading, passing, technique, crossing, and tackling. 

Goalkeeping: Handling and reflexes. 

Elite Training Schedule 

For top-tier clubs, you can push the players harder. Use Intensive settings for the primary role (e.g., Tactics for Defenders, Shooting for Strikers) and keep Fitness at Medium to avoid excessive injuries. 

Training for Small Teams 

If you are managing a lower-league side, avoid multiple “Intensive” categories. Focus on Medium for Fitness and Skills to keep your small squad available for match days. 

Pro Tip: Remove all players from training during the off-season. Re-assign them to their routines 1-2 weeks before the first pre-season friendly!
