IodineCF: The Legend 

If you play with the original database and choose a lower-league team or a club with a limited budget, IodineCF is the ultimate solution. 

This tactic optimizes player positioning in a way that the AI struggle to counter. 

When using IodineCF, competing on equal terms with giants like Real Madrid, Manchester United, or AC Milan is no longer a dream. 

It is so efficient that it might even make the game feel “too easy.” 

I have managed to win top-tier leagues and continental trophies using very average squads thanks to this setup. 

What about Guga Barça? 

The Guga Barça tactic is excellent but requires a bit more technical quality from your players to work perfectly. It focuses on ball retention and smart movement, meaning your squad needs decent passing and positioning attributes to really shine.
