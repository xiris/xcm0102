Re: Cm Scout: Scout % meaning 

Quote 

Post by Theov » Sun Sep 10, 2023 5:22 pm 

So, these are the attributes that don't change (through practice - they might change with age, but I don't think so, haven't tested this yet.) 
Anyway. 
So let's call these 'set' attributes. They don't change. 

Scope: to spot potential stars at younger age, we need to see which attributes correlate with high Scout%, since a high Scout% means a player will help a team most to win. And what is the minimum these attributes should be. 

Method: look at the current top players, according to Scout% and then look what their attributes are for these set attributes. I looked at the top 40 player in Scout%, regardless of position or age. 

Set attributes: 
Balance, aggression, Anticipation, Bravery, Determination, Influence, Work Ethic, Creativity, Flair. 

Balance: at least 9 is needed, no player had lower. Most had 12 though. So consider 12 the minimum, but 9 acceptable for otherwise excellent players. 
Aggression: no correlation 
Anticipation: 15 seemed to be the magic number. 
Bravery: all positions except SC or F this needs to be 12. 
Determination: 13 
Influence: no correlation 
Work ethic: 12 
Creativity: 12, especially for midfielders and forwards. Defenders and keepers don't need this stat. 
Flair: no correlation 

Later I will dive into the hidden attributes, but this is a good starting point for now.

Quote 

Post by Theov » Mon Sep 11, 2023 7:12 am 

Hidden attributes and how they relate to Scout% 

Attribute: required value found in top players 

Adaptability: no correlation, but important when buying foreigners 
Ambition: no correlation 
Consistency: 16 
Dirtiness: no correlation 
Imp Matches: 14 
Injury Proneness: self explanatory, it doesn't lead to better players, but they will be available more 
Natural Fitness: 13 
Loyalty: no correlation, but the more the better of course 
One on Ones: GK only, 13 
Professionalism: 13, or no correlation if other attributes are elite 
Corners: no correlation 
Sportsmanship: no correlation 
Temperament: no correlation with Scout% 

Potential Ability: the lowest on the list of 80+ Scout% was Papadopoulos and Tsigalko with PA 139. The magic number however seems to be 150. 

Conclusion: 

to find the young players with a high potential scout% you should look for: 
Balance: 9 
Anticipation: 15 
Bravery: 12, defenders and GKs 
Determination: 13 
Creativity: 12, midfielders and forwards 
Consistency: 16 
Imp Matches: 14 
Natural Fitness: 13 
One on Ones: 13, GKs 
Work rate: 12 

Optional per situation: 
Adapt: 7 
Inj: max 12 
Professionalism: 13
