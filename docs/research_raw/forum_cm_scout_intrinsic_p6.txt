I've been using CM Scout for some years now and always worked fine but out of curiosity I decided to test CM Scout Intrinsic in a new save. Maybe it's not the right topic to do it but I'll just do a quick overview and differences between the two Scouts. In CM Scout Intrinsic I'm using the values posted here by MadScientist. 

The app it self is Scout Intrinsic is newer, more intuitive, better looking than the CM Scout and in general is better to interact with but I find the options you have in CM Scout more complete, especially with contracts . You can see more details of it, use filters to see players with a release clause and etc. In the other hand in the Intrinsic Scout you have more information about the player, like the stats not only in the game but other values as the intrinsic and in match and about the position he plays better. 

One thing I noticed is CM Scout seems to be more aligned with the game in what is considered a good or bad player. Like, if you get a player with high scout rating in CM Scout and tries to sell him after more clubs are interested than the ones from Scout intrinsic. 

Last but not least, probably there is a reason wrote right here in this topic that I didn't read, or remember, but Scout Intrinsic seems to give much higher ratings for defenders and goalkeepers than strikers and middle fields. It seems more equal in CM Scout. 

In the top 50 players from Scout Intrinsic 25 are GK, 16 DC while just 3 middle fielders (2 defensive and one central midfield, none offensive) and 6 strikers (I'm considering Strikers and Forwards together). The first offensive middle to apear is in the 58th position. In CM Scout is 10 GK, 7 DC, 16 middle fielders (there is defensive, central and offensive ones) and 8 strikers. 

After this long post, witch one to use? I, personally, will be probably using both to find my players as both are good tools. If I had to chose one, I would probably stick to CM Scout as I use it for longer and I am way more adapted to it.

Post by finrod799 » Tue Dec 09, 2025 7:52 pm 

MadScientist wrote: Sun Aug 07, 2022 8:40 pm It depends. 

In game: displays the values as they are displayed in game (1 to 20) 

Intrinsic: useful to know the strengths and weaknesses of a player (-125 to +125). Usually remain static throughout the player career (unless he isnt in general training or the att is experience-related). Doesn't take CA into account. 

In match: displays the values approximatelly as seen by the match engine (0 to 45). Better to evaluate current attribute skill because takes CA into account and isnt clamped to 20 as the in game. 

The normalized versions just map it to 1-20 range based on the player with the highest att in the world at that moment. 
This!!! I'm glad I've managed to dig to this sentence. Now looking on CM source code everything makes sense. Rating is based on this normalized version that means that is fluctuating between saves a lot thus the population is changing. I could not understand why I buy player and after 2-3 saves his rating is dropping by 2-3 points although he is playing very well and other stats are even improving due to training. His normalized intrinsic version was down due the population was changing and even small change due to high weight in rating like Finishing for strikers like 30 000 was dragging him down.
