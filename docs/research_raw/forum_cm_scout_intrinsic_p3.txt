Usually, but not 100% 
The percentage tells you what position his attributes are best for, but there is a value from 1-20 for each side/centre and for each position GK-FWD. That determines where a player plays best. 

So someone who the game says is a D/DM R/C might actually have 15 for D and 20 for DM as well as 15 for C and 20 for R. They will be best as a DM R and worst as a DC no mater what the attributes say. 

So where do you find this ? Several ways, but the GK editor will tell you.

Which setting for CM scout inrinsic 

Quote 

Post by MadScientist » Sun Aug 07, 2022 8:40 pm 

Xazal wrote: Sun Aug 07, 2022 6:09 pm As the titel says which CA15 attribute View mode are u guys using? Which one is the most important one?! Im completely confused 
It depends. 

In game: displays the values as they are displayed in game (1 to 20) 

Intrinsic: useful to know the strengths and weaknesses of a player (-125 to +125). Usually remain static throughout the player career (unless he isnt in general training or the att is experience-related). Doesn't take CA into account. 

In match: displays the values approximatelly as seen by the match engine (0 to 45). Better to evaluate current attribute skill because takes CA into account and isnt clamped to 20 as the in game. 

The normalized versions just map it to 1-20 range based on the player with the highest att in the world at that moment.
